# Evidence ledger

Cutoff: 7 September 2026. Targeted Consensus discovery and fetched paper records were checked using publisher, PubMed and DOI metadata where available. This is a focused narrative evidence update, not a systematic review. Search themes: autism complexity/entropy, sham-controlled theta-burst stimulation, individual connectivity, adaptive stimulation, entropic brain theory and neural-mass modeling. Consensus may return preprints or incorrect year metadata; those labels were independently checked.

| Peer-reviewed source | What it contributes | Boundary on the claim |
|---|---|---|
| [Tan et al., 2026, BMJ](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2025-086295) | A positive multicenter sham-controlled motor-cortex stimulation trial in 200 children | Specific protocol; one-month follow-up; neither entropy targeting nor closed loop |
| [Ni et al., 2023, Autism Research](https://doi.org/10.1002/aur.2954) | No primary superiority of left-frontal cTBS in a 60-person trial | Does not invalidate every target or modality |

## Verification notes

The Lyons Consensus record carried a 2024 year; the publisher identifies the relevant article as 2026. Preprints encountered in discovery were not treated as peer-reviewed evidence. Moses, Doss and Tagliazucchi's 2026 critical review was discovered, but its content was not sufficiently accessible for substantive synthesis and it was not used to support claims. Citation counts were not used as quality scores.
